Descriptors. The neural arms concatenate the RDKit 2D physicochemical block (~200 descriptors) to the learned graph embedding at the feed-forward layer; the tree and kernel arms use that block plus count-based Morgan and FCFP fingerprints and MACCS keys; the published-protocol arm uses PaDEL fingerprints as published. Non-finite columns and columns exceeding 1E10 are dropped before fitting. Software: ChemProp 2.2.3 with PyTorch Lightning, RDKit (Landrum, rdkit.org), PaDEL (Yap, J. Comput. Chem. 2011, 32, 1466–1474), LightGBM, scikit-learn, Schrödinger LigPrep; training on an NVIDIA T4.

Loss and selection. ST-RAE charges nothing inside a compound's credible interval, so the training loss carries a matching band term per labelled cell, in scaled target space: L = α·[relu(pred − (y+hi)) + relu((y−lo) − pred)] + (1−α)·(pred − y)², with α = 0.7 and hi, lo that cell's own asymmetric half-widths. Arms were accepted per isoform on a fixed gate of mean error, 90th-percentile error and ranking against two baselines.

Label interpretation.

CensoringDose–response readings at or below the top screening concentration (pIC50 4.305) establish an upper bound only, and are left-censored under BoundedMSE
MissingnessAbout one third of screened compounds received a curve; for CYP2D6 the curves were run preferentially on apparent hits, so all 1,493 CYP2D6 labels are inhibitors while 2,883 inactive compounds carry an empty cell
Pseudo-labels (tier S)The curve-fitter's fallback value is a software floor, agreeing across isoforms to within 0.04 log units (1A2 2.168 · 2C9 2.333 · 2D6 2.168 · 3A4 2.206). Each isoform's own floor is assigned to every compound in the clearly-inactive zone of the screen: 2,687 cells added, 2,424 of them CYP2D6 non-inhibitors, contamination 2.3%, mean error 0.168. CYP2D6 labels 1,493 → 3,917
Pseudo-label treatmentEntered as point estimates, not censored bounds: censoring is enabled for real measurements and disabled for pseudo rows, with a hard abort if the CYP2D6 prediction standard deviation falls below 0.5
External dataA 30,980-row union corpus was assembled and not used: ChEMBL CYP potency carries a +0.45–0.65 log offset and a hard floor at 4.00 against this assay. Where external data was used in development it was carried on separate heads, never merged into a scored target
