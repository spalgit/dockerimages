1. Models

Models were built per isoform: CYP2D6 and CYP3A4 are independent runs with their own hyperparameters, blend weight and decision threshold. Selection was on MCC under Bemis–Murcko scaffold splits.

Descriptors. The in-house arms use the RDKit 2D physicochemical block (~200 descriptors) concatenated with count-based Morgan and FCFP fingerprints, MACCS keys and a hand-curated MBI structural-alert count vector; non-finite columns and columns exceeding 1E10 are dropped before fitting. The published-protocol grid uses PaDEL fingerprints exactly as published. Software: ChemProp, LightGBM, XGBoost, RDKit (Landrum, rdkit.org), PaDEL (Yap, J. Comput. Chem. 2011, 32, 1466–1474), scikit-learn (Pedregosa et al., JMLR 2011, 12, 2825–2830) and Optuna (Akiba et al., KDD 2019, 2623–2631).

3. Ligand preparation and labels

Ligand preparation, applied identically to the training and blind sets. The six RDKit corrections run in the order shown, through a single driver.

#StepDetail
0Schrödinger LigPrepDesalting, neutralisation, tautomer and ionisation enumeration at pH 7.4
1Sulfonamide NR-SO2-[N−]-R′ → R-SO2-NH-R′
2Pyridinium N[nH+] → n; quaternary [n+] left unchanged
3Morpholine N[NH+] → N
4Iminium NAmidine / guanidine / iminium C=[NH+] → C=N, sp2 nitrogen only
5AmideN=C([O-]) → NC(=O), bond orders corrected
6Phenolc[O-] → cOH
7Artefact fallbackUnrepairable LigPrep artefacts revert to the raw input SMILES; raw, LigPrep and cleaned SMILES retained side by side, so the step is idempotent. Charged species 19.2% → 14.6%
8FeaturiserLargest fragment selected, then RDKit Uncharger: graph and descriptor block are computed on neutral structures

Label interpretation. is_TDI is a deterministic function of two measured potency surfaces, verified to reproduce the published label on every training row carrying both.

Definitionis_TDI ⟺ max(pIC50_TDI_condition, 4.0) − max(pIC50_direct, 4.0) > log10(2)
Negative classAny compound whose shifted curve never clears 4.301, so part of the class is compounds whose potency was never established rather than demonstrated non-inhibitors — 34.8% of the CYP3A4 negatives
CensoringLeft-censored potency rows treated as upper bounds, not as points
CYP2D6 coverageNo labelled compound in the saturated zone of the single-shot screen (curves were run only on pre-flagged compounds); that region filled with pseudo-negatives derived from the isoforms with data there, contamination 1 positive in 836 zone compounds
