1. Methods

No receptor structure is provided, so each complex is produced from sequence and SMILES. Two pose sources were built — co-folding and ensemble docking into the co-folded receptors — and the submitted archives draw from both.

Support RDKit 2025.3.6 / 2025.9.6 (ligand handling, SMARTS, atom naming); gemmi 0.7.4 / 0.7.5 (CIF and PDB I/O); MDAnalysis 2.9.0 (challenge validator) Archive construction and validation respective projects

Hardware. Co-folding on an NVIDIA Tesla T4 (16 GB) at one worker; docking on a local RTX 4060 at 16 workers, ~25 runs per minute free and ~3× that cost per pinned run. A 503-residue complex with haem is ~4,100 atoms and needs 6–10 GB VRAM per worker.

Protonation tool choicedimorphite-dl not used: at pH 7.4 it returns protonated amide, urea and sulfonamide nitrogens and a net +3 charge on one ligand
Coordinating-atom selectionPer-ligand smallest rooted-environment SMARTS. gnina pins the first atom of the first SMARTS match and cannot pin by index, so each pattern is rooted at the intended nitrogen
ReceptorUniProt P08684 canonical sequence, 503 residues, with a 14,302-sequence MSA and no structural template; haem B supplied as the CCD HEM entry
Docking receptorsEach of the 300 co-folded complexes stripped to protein plus haem, its own ligand discarded
